What is a deck

A deck is a folder of words. Decks can be nested, so you can build a tree based on where your words come from, for example by show, season and episode: 

📂 CzechEnglish (EN → CS)
   📂 Two and half men
      📂 Series 1
         📄 Episode 1
         📄 Episode 2
📄 Španělština (ES → CS)
  • The tree can have at most {{ max_depth }} levels.
  • Each deck can have both words and sub-decks at the same time.
  • A name must be unique within one folder, and letter case does not matter (HIMYM = himym).

New deck and languages

  1. On the Overview, click + New deck.
  2. Fill in the name and the languages:
    • I want to learn: the language of the words, e.g. English,
    • I speak: the language you translate the words into, e.g. Czech. It is prefilled from Settings.

Only a top-level deck has a language pair, shown next to its name, e.g. (EN → CS). Sub-decks inherit it, and it cannot be changed later.

To add a sub-deck, open the deck and click + New next to the Sub-decks heading.

Moving by drag and drop

To move a deck on the Overview, drag it. On a phone, press and hold it for a moment first.

Grab the deck and drag it. The rows do not move while you drag; you only see where the deck will land:

  • onto the middle of a row: the row is highlighted and the deck goes inside it, even into a deck that has no sub-decks yet,
  • near the top or bottom edge of a row: a line appears and the deck is placed before or after it. This is how you change the order and also move a deck to the top level.

With a mouse, just drag. On a phone, press and hold the deck for a moment first, then drag. A quick swipe only scrolls the page.

The move is saved as soon as you let go. It is not possible when:

  • the target folder has different languages,
  • the deck would end up inside itself or inside one of its sub-decks,
  • the tree would have more than {{ max_depth }} levels,
  • the target folder already has a deck with the same name.

In that case the deck goes back to where it was and the reason is shown above the tree.

More options on the deck page

  • Practice deck: a test from this deck and its sub-decks only.
  • ⋯ next to the deck name opens a menu: Edit name and description, Move and Delete.
  • Edit name and description: the name and the description.
  • Move: move by choosing from a list; only places where the deck can go are offered. Also handy if you use the keyboard.
  • Arrows ↑ ↓ next to sub-decks: change the order without dragging.
  • Delete: deletes the deck and its sub-decks, plus the words that are not in any other deck. Words that are also elsewhere stay. Before deleting, you see how much of each will be removed.
