Layout

The app looks the same on a computer and on a phone: one column, with sections as cards. At the top there is a bar with the logo (a ghost peeking out from behind a card – click it to go back to the overview) Vocaboo · ▶︎ Practice · Statistics · Help (on a phone, Statistics and Help are in the account menu, and the bar keeps only the logo, ▶︎ Practice and the circle) and a circle with the first letter of your email – click it to open a menu with your email, (on a phone) 📊 Statistics and ❓ Help, the theme switch ☀️ Light · 🌙 Dark · Auto (Auto follows your system setting; the choice applies only in this browser), Settings and Log out. The ▶︎ Practice button in the bar starts a test from anywhere. On a phone you can put the app on your home screen, see The app on the home screen. The overview has the cards What you struggle with today, Decks (with a + New link for a new deck) and Today.

Numbers on the overview

number in brackets next to a deck, e.g. HIMYM (26)words in the deck and its subdecks
↻ and a smaller number in brackets, e.g. HIMYM (26 ↻ 9)how many words of the deck (and its subdecks) are due now; when nothing is waiting, the brackets show only the number of words (on hover: “9 to review”)

Below the decks is Today: only what you achieved in tests today. Each number is also a switch: click it to show a bar chart of the last 7 days below the numbers (a number above each bar, other days in light indigo, today in strong indigo).

  • 🎯 right for the first time: words answered 😎 or 🤔 today for the first time ever, in any direction (each word once).
  • 🎓 words learned: words that today became “known” for the first time, that is, right twice in a row in every direction it was tested in (the same as in Statistics). The second right answer comes only after an interval (7 days after 😎), so a new word shows up here a few days later at the earliest.
  • 📚 words in tests: how many different words were in tests today; a word in both directions counts once.
  • ⏱️ time in tests: how long your tests took today (up to the last answer).

At the very top (above the decks) is the What you struggle with today section, so the words stay in sight: up to 3 smaller flip cards in one row, based only on today's answers: 🐢 You think longest about, 🦀 Only one way and 🐘 Over and over – a word you got wrong today that has the most tries in its history (at least 3), e.g. “don't know again · try no. 12”. A word like that needs a different approach, such as a sentence or a memory trick. If you had no test today or everything went well, the section is not shown. Statistics starts with the same three cards, followed by more (up to 10 cards), for the last 30 days.

Statistics

The Statistics link in the top bar opens an overview of your learning:

  • Tiles at the top: total words (and how many were added this week), how many you know (right at least twice in a row in every direction the word was tested in; a direction not tested yet does not count), 🔥 how many days in a row you have been learning and your longest streak, and the number of answers.
  • Words over time: how many words you have in total, from the day of your first word. The step depends on how long you have been learning: by day for the first 2 weeks, by week up to 4 months, by month up to 2 years, then by quarter. The numbers on the left show the count; hover over a dot to see the exact value.
  • Recent tests: your last 10 tests, one per row: when it was (date and time), the path to the deck, last 3 levels (e.g. … › Series › HIMYM › S01; hover to see the full path), the direction (e.g. EN → CS, CS → EN or EN → CS + CS → EN for both directions), scope and limit, number of words, success rate (the share of 😎 and 🤔 as a percentage and a small bar; hover over the bar or open the test summary to see the counts of 😎 🤔 😕 😵) and the length of the test (⏹ for an unfinished test). With both directions each word is in the test twice, once in each direction, and each one is rated separately. Above the table you see how many tests you did in 14 days. Click a row to open the test summary. Tests without a single answer are not shown.
  • What you struggle with: words you struggle with, as cards like in a test, so you keep learning even while looking at your statistics. The front shows an animal with the category and one side of the word (half of the cards show the foreign word, half the translation; for 🦀 always the direction you struggle with). Tap the card (or press Enter) to flip it: you see the other side, why the word is there, and a Details link. It is based on answers from the last 30 days, so the cards keep changing.

You “struggle” with a word when it got the answer 😵 No idea or 😕 I half remember in that period and you don't know it yet (in some direction it does not have two right answers in a row). Each word is shown only once:

  • 🦈 Worst enemy: the most “no idea” answers, e.g. “3× don't know at all out of 5 tries”.
  • 🦀 Only one way: you know it in one direction but not the other, e.g. “EN → CS yes, CS → EN no”.
  • 🐿️ You forget: a word that was right at least twice in a row and then got a mistake, e.g. “2× forgotten”.
  • 🐒 Roller coaster: the word that switches the most between “know” and “don't know” (at least 3 switches), e.g. “4× switch know ↔ don't know”.
  • 🐇 Too fast: the fastest “no idea” (under 3 s), e.g. “1.2 s and don't know at all” – you are probably guessing this word rather than thinking.
  • 🦎 You mix up: two words with the same translation that you both struggle with, e.g. borrow · lend – půjčit.
  • 🦒 Longest you struggle with
  • 🐢 You think longest about: the longest average time before you flip the card.
  • 🐌 Learning it longest: the word you have had the longest and still struggle with, e.g. “added 45 days ago”.

Word details

Click a word in a deck's list to open its details. Under the word, its translation and the buttons comes Reviews. On the right of its heading is the status of the word with a coloured dot; hover to see how many times it was in a test (You know it when every direction it was tested in was right at least twice in a row, otherwise Learning, and New when it has not been in a test yet, the same as in Statistics; hover to see when a word counts as known). Below it, one direction under the other (e.g. EN → CS and CS → EN):

  • Next review: when the word comes again (now, in 3 days); hover to see the exact date and how many times in a row you were right. A word that has not been in a test yet only says New, not in a test yet.
  • Chart of answers: up to the last 30 answers on a time axis from the left, oldest first, each as a dot in the colour of its rating, higher is better (😵 at the bottom, then 😕, 🤔, 😎). The line between them shows whether you are improving: it goes up when the word goes better and better. Answers of one day stand next to each other (also when the word came several times in a test), a pause longer than a week is shortened and the line over it is dashed. Both directions share one time axis: the same day is at the same place in both charts. Under the lower chart is the date when each learning period started and, at the right end, the date of the last answer. Hover over a dot or hold your finger on it to see the date and the rating. With one or two answers there are only dots, no line.

Below it In decks (all decks the word is in, with the sentence), Other meanings from the dictionary and at the end the folded Answer history: click it to see every answer (when, direction, rating as a smiley, thinking time).

Settings

Settings are grouped by where they take effect in the app:

  • App › App language: the language of menus, buttons and help (Čeština, English, Español, or Same as the browser). It applies on all devices where you log in. Your words and decks do not change. Before you log in, you can switch the language below the login form.
  • App › Put Vocaboo on your home screen: the Install button puts the app on the home screen of your phone or on your computer, see The app on the home screen. It is shown only where the browser can do it and the app is not on the home screen yet.
  • Decks › I speak: your language, filled in for a new deck.
  • Adding words › Suggest translations: suggestions from the dictionary below the translation field. On by default.
  • Test › Default test direction: filled in when you start a test.
  • Test › Show other meanings from the dictionary: other meanings from the dictionary at the bottom of the back of the card (as many as fit on a line, the rest after clicking +N). Your own meanings are always shown. On by default.
  • Test › Audio › Play automatically: the foreign word is read aloud by itself – in the foreign → native direction as soon as the card appears, in the native → foreign direction after you click Show. You choose either the word or the whole sentence (without a sentence, the word is read). The browser won't read the very first card of a test until you click on the page for the first time. On by default. 🔊 is always on the card and can't be turned off.
  • Test › Show written pronunciation (IPA): below the foreign word on the card and in the word details, e.g. [pɑːm]. Off by default.
  • Test › Show when the word comes again: under the rating buttons of a test, e.g. 10 min, 1 day, 3 days. Off by default.
  • Account › Change password: enter your current password and the new one twice. The eye icon on the right of the field shows the password, a second click hides it again (the same when logging in). You stay logged in on this device; on other devices you will need to log in again. A forgotten password can be reset on the login page with Forgot your password?: enter your email and you get a link to set a new password (it works for 24 hours).

Open settings

Account

Create an account on the login page with Create an account: enter your email and your password twice (and, when the app asks for it, the invitation code from the person who invited you). A six-digit code comes to your email; enter it on the next page. It works for 15 minutes and Send a new code sends another one. Then the account is ready and you are logged in. No email? Look into spam too. If you close the page with the code, just log in later: the app sends a new code and opens the page again. An account whose code is not entered within 7 days is deleted.

The app on the home screen

You can put Vocaboo on the home screen of your phone (or on your computer) as an app: it opens with one tap, on the whole screen and without the browser bar. The icon is the same ghost on an indigo square. Nothing is downloaded from a store; it is still the same app with the same data.

  • Android, Chrome or Edge on a computer: in Settings › App tap Install and confirm it in the browser's window.
  • iPhone and iPad: Apple does not allow installing with a button, so Install shows the steps: tap Share (the square with an arrow up; if you don't see it, tap ⋯ first), then Add to Home Screen and Add. On an iPhone the app on the home screen has its own login, so log in there once more.
  • Android with Firefox and similar browsers (they have no install window): Install shows the steps: in the browser menu ⋮ (at the top right, in Firefox it can be at the bottom) choose Install app or Add to Home screen and confirm.

On a phone the overview offers it once by itself too (the card Put Vocaboo on your home screen); after Not now it does not come back, the offer in Settings stays. When the app is on the home screen already, or the browser can't do it (e.g. Firefox on a computer), the offer in Settings is not there. You stay logged in for 90 days since your last visit.

Multiple devices

Everything is stored on the server. When you log in on both your phone and your computer, you see the same decks and the same review progress everywhere.
