Adding a word

You add words on the deck detail page. Only the first two fields are required:

FieldExampleNote
foreign languagehello; hiseparate more variants with a semicolon
your languageahoj; čausame here
SentenceHello, kids.under the + sentence, note button, where the word appeared (under the foreign word)
Sentence translationAhoj, děti.same place, optional, helps you understand the sentence (under the translation). You can mark the word with braces: Utekl {pryč}.
Notesame place, anything extra

Don't write the season or episode anywhere, the deck sets it: add words from the first episode to the deck Series 1 › Episode 1.

Enter submits the form (like the Add word button, which says Adding… while it is sending). Then the form clears and the cursor jumps back to the first field, so you can type the next word right away. You can tell the word was added by the line ✓ Added: word → translation above the fields (so you see a typo in the translation at once), which lights up for 2 seconds. If the word stays in the fields and nothing lit up, it was not added.

Search

On the overview, in the Decks tab, there is a light Search words… bar above the tree: click it (on a computer you can also press /) and type, the results show up right below it.

  • It searches all your decks: the foreign word and the translation (all variants), the sentence and its translation, and the note.
  • Accents and capital letters don't matter: “cetka” also finds “Četka”. Results show up as you type, from 2 letters.
  • First come the words where the word or translation matches, then the ones where the text is in the sentence or note (the sentence is shown under the word). The match is highlighted.
  • Each word shows which decks it is in (the last 3 levels of the path, the full path when you hover over it). Click a word to open its detail, ✎ to edit it right away.
  • At most 50 results are shown.

Pending

Watching a film, you hear a word, don't know what it means and the suggestions don't know it either? Save it without a translation and come back to it later:

  • In a deck: type the foreign word (and maybe a sentence and a note, e.g. the series and episode). When the suggestions don't know the typed word, their row shows The suggestions don't know this word · 📝 Save for later. Tap it and the word is saved without a translation.
  • Pending words are not in tests, nor in the counts of the tree and the statistics, until they have a translation.
  • The overview has a card Pending (“3 words are waiting for a translation” and the first two words) with the link Fill in. The same card is on the deck page under the form; there the two words are links that fill in the form. On the Pending page tap a word: its deck opens with the usual form, the word, the sentence and the note are filled in (the field with the word lights up for a moment) and the cursor waits in the translation. When the dictionary of the app knows the word by then, the suggestions show at once. After Add word the word is in the deck (and comes in tests), it leaves the list and the link Next pending takes you to the next one.
  • Words the suggestions don't know go into the next extension of the app's dictionary.

Words from a topic

Ideal when you have no time to look up and write down words, or don't know where to start: click + From a topic next to the heading New word on the deck page (only for English → Czech decks). Choose a topic (for example Nature › Animals, or a whole group Nature (all)), levels (A1–C2, A1–B1 by default) and parts of speech (nouns, verbs, adjectives, other; all by default — for an Animals deck maybe only nouns) and click Show words. You'll see the most common words of the topic with a translation and a sentence, 20 on a page.

  • Tick the ones you want to learn and click + Add ticked words. They are added to the deck as your own words, with the sentence and its translation; you can then edit them like any other. You stay on the same page and the added words disappear from it.
  • Words you already have in this deck are not offered. The ones you have in another deck are hidden, so a second deck of the same topic starts with new words; above the list you see how many words the topic has, how many of them are already in your other decks and in this deck, and a link Show them (then they are greyed out with the name of the deck and cannot be ticked).
  • Select all on this page ticks all 20 at once; then you can untick what you don't want. The button shows how many are ticked.
  • We recommend smaller decks of about 20 words: they are easier to learn. Add the next ones when you know these. You cannot add more than one page at once.

Translation suggestions

When you type a foreign word, translation suggestions show up under the translation field with a part-of-speech abbreviation, for example present → současnost (n.), dárek (n.), představit (v.). If they don't fit on one line, they continue on the next one. Click a suggestion to put it in the translation field and the line clears, then just press Enter. When the app dictionary has a sentence for this meaning, it also fills in the sentence with its translation (only if there is no sentence yet) and you'll see it under the Add word button, for example “Let's take the elevator.” · Pojeďme výtahem. Click edit to open it so you can change or delete it, done closes it again. Without a sentence and note, you'll see + sentence, note there.

  • The suggestions come from the app's own English–Czech dictionary (about 10,000 of the most common words and phrases, sorted by frequency). For other words nothing shows up and you write the translation by hand. There is no dictionary for Spanish yet.
  • You can edit a suggestion after it's inserted, nothing is saved on its own.
  • If you change the foreign word after picking a suggestion, the sentence filled in from the suggestion disappears, because it belonged to the previous word. Your own (written or edited) sentence is not deleted.
  • Suggestions also show up when you edit a word (even during a test via ✎), right away for the word in the form. Clicking a suggestion replaces the translation and fills the sentence only into empty fields.
  • If you don't want suggestions, turn them off in Settings (Adding words › Suggest translations).
  • Only the meaning you pick is saved. You'll see the other meanings from the app dictionary on the back of the card and in the word detail.

Variants (synonyms)

hello; hi ↔ ahoj; čau is one word with two variants on each side. In a test, any of them is correct.

Variants mean that each one on the left matches each one on the right. If that's not true, better make two separate words. For example hi ≠ dobrý den, so: hello → dobrý den; ahoj and hi → ahoj; čau (informal).

Sentence

Write the sentence normally, the app finds the word in it by itself and highlights it:

You typeWhat happens
She can read from her palm.the word palm is found automatically, the usual case
She has small {palms}.mark a word in a different form with curly braces, otherwise it isn't found
He {ran} away.for the word run: past tense in braces
She can read from her {}.empty braces = the word from the first field (palm)

The braces are never shown. In a test, the sentence looks like this:

palm → ?

palm

“She can read from her palm.”

dlaň → ?

dlaň

“She can read from her ___.”

In the direction from your language, the whole sentence would give away the answer, so the front shows it with a gap. The full sentence is on the back. If the word can't be found in the sentence, the sentence is only on the back.

More meanings (palm, present)

Each meaning is a separate word. The app tells them apart by the translation, you don't need to fill in anything else:

ForeignTranslationSentence
palmdlaňShe read my palm.
palmpalmaA tall palm on the beach.
presentdárekI bought her a present.
presentpřítomnostLive in the present.
presentprezentovatHe {presented} the project.
  • The same foreign word with the same translation is a duplicate. With a different translation it's another meaning and it gets saved.
  • In the test palm → ? the sentence helps. Without a sentence you'll see (2 meanings) under the word, so you know there's a choice.
  • Other meanings from the app dictionary and your other words with the same word are on the back of the card and in the word detail. There, meanings from the app dictionary have a + Learn this meaning too button.

Duplicate check

While you type, the app looks for words you already have and offers what to do with them:

You typeYou already haveIt offers

hey → ahojhello → ahojAdd “hey” as a variant of “hello”, or save it separately
palm → palmapalm → dlaňa notice about a different translation: same meaning (add as a variant), or a different meaning (just save)
caučaujust a notice Similar (without accents); byt and být are different words

The Add word button is blocked as long as saving would create a duplicate.

One word in several decks

The word belongs to you, decks only link to it. When hello is in 20 episodes, a test from the whole series asks it once and the rating counts in all decks. But each deck keeps its own sentence.

Editing and removing

  • Click a word in the list to open its detail, from there ✎ Edit (under the word and its translation, next to 🔊 Play) or, in the ⋯ menu, Remove from this deck.
  • Changes to variants and the note apply in all decks, the sentence only in that one.
  • Move (in the ⋯ menu in the word detail) moves it to another deck.
  • Report a mistake (in the ⋯ menu in the word detail): when a translation or a sentence from the suggestions is wrong, choose Wrong suggested translation, Wrong suggested sentence or Something else and, if you like, write a note, for example the right translation. The report helps to fix the dictionary of the app; your word does not change, fix it with ✎ Edit. When the word is reported already, the page says so and you can change the report. You can also report it right in a test with the ⚐ button under the card.
  • Removing a word from a deck deletes it only there. If it was its last deck, it's deleted completely, including its review history; the app warns you first.

Moving several words

Did words end up in the wrong deck, for example in Series 1 instead of Episode 1?

  1. In the word list, click Select and checkboxes appear (All selects all of them).
  2. At the bottom, click Move… and pick the target deck. Only decks with the same languages are offered.
  3. Move moves the words, Add there too keeps them in both decks.

The sentence and learning progress stay. If the word is already in the target deck, it isn't duplicated, only its sentence is filled in there if it was missing.
